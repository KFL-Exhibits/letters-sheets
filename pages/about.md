## About The Class

The seed for this exhibit was the final projects from ENGL 3025 taught during the Fall 2025 semester. The theme of the class was Epistolary Writing. Students developed these collections with a broad charge: letters could be anything from formal letters to napkin notes to postcards. The purpose of the project was to analyze both the idea of a collection and the idea of a letter through a rhetorical lens.

In this exhibit, you are invited to contribute too. If these letters inspire you, add your own letter at the "Write" Tab or [at this link](https://forms.gle/8dRS11P8AXQKRjdu6).
